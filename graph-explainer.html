What this path teaches

  • Edges are undirected evidence summaries with a direction label. Diuretic → AKI has 35 findings; AKI → diuretic has 4 more (the extractor read the arrow the other way). Directions can be wrong, so treat them cautiously.
  • The concept level loses context. Heat → AKI looks like 3 findings here, yet name-matching on "heat wave" and similar wording finds about 14 studies, because many are filed under generic concepts like "mortality rate" or "emergency department".
  • The AKI evidence comes from different worlds. The diuretic studies are hospital patients (surgery, transplant, cancer therapy). The heat studies are population time-series and heat stroke admissions. Nothing says the two apply to the same person, which is exactly the inference you would be making and flagging.
  • The gap is the finding. The dashed line is not in the graph. Your map's job is to make "drug → effect ← heat" legible and mark where the direct link is missing.
  • Self-loops exist (e.g. "lithium → lithium", 34 findings): findings comparing two variables of the same concept, such as lithium dose vs lithium level. Ignore them when drawing.

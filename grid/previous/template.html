How a cell is graded

Direct. At least one finding links this drug, or its class, to a heat outcome.
Bridged, strong. 30 or more studies link the drug to the cell's main mechanism, and 10 or more link heat to it.
Bridged, moderate. At least 10 drug studies and 3 heat studies.
Bridged, weak. Both halves are present, but below those thresholds.
Partial. Drug → mechanism is present, but no study links heat to the mechanism.
Gap. The action comes from drug labels or heat guidance. The graph has no study for its main mechanism.

What the counts are

  • Distinct studies in the System Public Graph v1 Dataverse release. That release covers 2.0M findings, already screened by System.
  • Each cell is graded on its main mechanism, the first one listed in its drawer. Other mechanisms are shown for context but do not raise the grade.
  • Drug → mechanism studies come from any setting, not only heat. Mechanisms are matched on variable names, so treat the counts as indicative.
  • Solid counts come from the Dataverse release, which System has vetted. "+N unvetted" counts come from the live API, where System estimates about 20% of rows are mis-extracted. The API was searched for heat, sweating and thermoregulation, lithium levels, dehydration and sodium.
  • Direct evidence is found three ways: a heat variable paired with a drug variable; a drug finding inside a paper whose title or abstract is about heat (seeded from 1,934 PubMed papers); and a heat finding inside a population defined by a drug, such as insulin users.
  • Recall check: of 13 known heat-and-medication papers with findings in the graph, the search finds 10. The other 3 have no drug named in any extracted finding.
  • Bridge counts keep only findings where the drug is the exposure and the mechanism the outcome, in people (no animal or poisoning studies).
  • Within a list, a finding is marked ● when its confidence interval excludes no effect, or, with no interval given, p < 0.05.

Before using this

  • Action wording is a draft for clinician and pharmacist review. It is not a protocol.
  • No study shown has been checked against its abstract yet. Each one carries an unchecked badge until it is.
  • Storage advice comes from drug labels. The graph has almost nothing on drug stability.

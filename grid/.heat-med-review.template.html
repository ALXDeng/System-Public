Grading
  • Moderate: 2+ heat-exposure studies, 1+ vetted.
  • Low: 1 vetted heat or lab-heat study.
  • Very low: seasonal, class-wide, unvetted or mechanism-only.
  • No effect: studied in heat, no difference. Not proof of safety.
  • None: label or pharmacology only.
  • Observational unless marked as a trial.
Limits
  • Actions are drafts. A forecast alone is not a reason to change a medication.
  • No study tests any action here.
  • Unvetted rows: ~20% mis-extracted. None hand-checked.
  • Mechanism examples exclude non-ambient heat and reversed effects.
  • Agency guidance not yet reviewed.

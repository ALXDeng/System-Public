      How to read this

      • Research marks show the strongest evidence for the class across all four actions (dose, hold, monitor, storage). "Mechanism only" means the drug and heat each link to the same mechanism in separate studies. It is plausible, not measured.
      • Label marks come from automated text matching across every manufacturer label openFDA returned for that drug (the share is shown in the detail). A dot is filled when at least half the labels say it. "Drug fever only" means the label lists hyperthermia as a symptom of serotonin syndrome, NMS or overdose, which is not a hot-weather warning.
      • Guidance was coded by hand from seven sources on 14 Nov 2026, by one coder. NYC and Arizona copy the CDC list, so the three US sources are close to one source. Germany's Heidelberg table and Australian state guidance were not included.
      • No study has been checked against its abstract yet. Unvetted API rows (System estimates about 20% are mis-extracted) are marked with a dotted ring.
      • A silent label or a null study is not evidence of safety.
